Burger

Our burger is served in a large, authentic American-style portion that can easily make you feel full on its own, with fresh ingredients. The seasoning and sauces are carefully developed and prepared by a chef with 15 years of experience.

Black n' Bleu Mushroom Burger
Smoky chargrilled beef with earthy mushrooms and sharp blue cheese. Rich, savory, slightly tangy with creamy and crispy onion contrast.

Surf & Turf Burger
Juicy grilled beef paired with crispy beer-battered shrimp. Bold, savory-meaty with a light sweet seafood note and crunchy texture.

Chicken Bacon Mushroom Burger
Tender grilled chicken with smoky bacon and earthy mushrooms. Savory, slightly smoky and creamy with nutty cheese and garlic aioli.

Philly Cheese Steak
Thin-sliced beef with sautéed onions and mushrooms. Deeply savory, rich umami flavor with creamy melted four-cheese sauce.

Burger

We understand that kids love burgers, but don’t mistake us for fast food. Every ingredient is carefully selected, must be fresh, and quality-checked, ensuring a dish that kids enjoy while parents can feel reassured.

California Chicken Burger
Chargrilled chicken with creamy smashed avocado, fried egg, and cheddar. Fresh, rich and creamy with a mix of buttery, savory and slightly smoky flavors.

Barnyard Burger
Chargrilled U.S. beef with bacon, cheddar, and fried egg. Bold, hearty and savory with smoky, salty richness and a soft-yolk creamy finish.

Buffalo Chicken Sandwich
Crispy battered chicken coated in spicy Buffalo sauce with bleu cheese. Spicy, tangy and savory with a crispy bite and cooling creamy contrast.

Pasta

Spaghetti Bolognese
Classic spaghetti with rich meaty tomato sauce and Parmesan. Savory, slightly tangy and hearty with deep umami from slow-cooked beef.

Steak Fajita Pasta

Chicken & Mushroom Alfredo Pasta

Creamy Pesto Shrimp Pasta
Pan-seared shrimp with spaghetti in creamy pesto sauce and Parmesan. Fresh basil aroma, nutty richness and a light seafood sweetness with creamy texture.

Steak

Steak is available everywhere, but here you can choose from six signature sauces based on the restaurant’s own recipes. The steak is grilled, carrying a subtle smoky aroma, while remaining refined, flavorful, and easy to enjoy.

Ribeye (200g)
Rich, well-marbled U.S. ribeye with a juicy, beefy and slightly buttery flavor. Served with roasted baby potatoes and steamed vegetables for a balanced, hearty plate.

Filet Mignon (180g)
Lean and extremely tender U.S. tenderloin with a mild, delicate beef flavor and soft buttery texture. Served with mashed potatoes, sautéed mushrooms, and asparagus for a smooth, elegant balance.

Pub Steak (200g)
U.S. top blade steak with a firm bite and bold, beefy flavor. Slightly smoky and savory, paired with crispy fries and grilled vegetables for a classic, satisfying pub-style meal.

Loaded Chili Fries

Spicy Buffalo Chicken Nachos

Texas Nachos
