FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Is S&L's Diner suitable for families?

S&L's Diner is a family-friendly restaurant offering a wide variety of dishes, from fried and grilled items to salads and desserts. Its authentic American-style food is especially popular with younger diners, while the depth of flavor and professional preparation also appeal to older guests. Our chef is always happy to accommodate small adjustments to dishes to better suit seniors. The spacious setting, warm decoration, and light background music create a comfortable and relaxed atmosphere for family meals.

What makes S&L's Diner different from other Western restaurants in Hanoi?

S&L's Diner is the first and only American diner in Hanoi, established in 2016 and led by American chef Steven Kaczerski, who brings over 15 years of experience. Our menu features not only classic dishes prepared with authentic flavors, such as nachos, burgers, and steak, but also harder-to-find items like milkshakes, chicken & waffles, and fluffy pancakes. We also serve breakfast. Beyond the food, we pride ourselves on being a restaurant that listens and adapts. We actively collect customer feedback after each meal, and if you’re not satisfied, we offer complimentary fries or ice cream as an apologize.

What are S&L's Diner’s opening hours?

S&L's Diner serves All-Day Breakfast throughout its opening hours, including pancakes, waffles, biscuits and gravy, and classic American egg dishes. The restaurant is open 365 days a year, from 11:00 AM to 10:00 PM, including holidays and Tet. Guests can dine in or order takeaway, with meals carefully wrapped in foil to maintain quality.
